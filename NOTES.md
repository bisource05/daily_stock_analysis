## What it is

A scheduled weekday dashboard sent to Telegram, covering a score, trend, buy/sell levels, risk alerts, catalysts and a checklist, for US, Hong Kong, China, Japan, Korea, Taiwan and ETF tickers, using free data sources by default.

## Caution

Read the risk alerts before the score. Free data sources can be rate-limited or change without warning, so add a paid data source if this becomes a daily dependency.

## Status

Forked Oct 27, 2026. Nothing here has been run, configured, or connected to any account, API key, or real money. Paper / dry-run first, and only after a walk-forward backtest with fees, a look-ahead check, and 30 days on paper.
